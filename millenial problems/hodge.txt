  Diagnosing Hodge Conjecture
  ### 1. Setup & Mapping (Checking Pre-conditions)

  Ontological Sieve (Set-Theoretic):
  • Ambient Set (U): The continuous vector space of de Rham cohomology H²ᵏ(X, ℂ) varying over the moduli space of complex structures.
  • Closure Operator (cl): Rational linear span to isolate the discrete algebraic lattice H²ᵏ(X, ℚ).
  • Well-Order (≺): Continuous analytic deformations (the Gauss-Manin connection) varying the Hodge filtration H^{p,q}.
  • Möbius Inversion: Tracks the dimensional jumps of the intersection H^{k,k} ∩ H²ᵏ(X, ℚ).
  • Adaptive Gradients: The gradient is continuous (complex moduli), but the target is a rigid discrete lattice.

  Categorical Machine (Abelian/Structural):

  • Ambient Category (𝒜): Coh(X), the Grothendieck category of coherent sheaves on a smooth projective complex variety X.
  • Target Object (U₀): The structure sheaf 𝒪_X, representing the continuous geometry, whose algebraic cycles generate the Hodge classes via the Chern character.
  • Semi-Artinian Condition: Fails. The variety X has dimension ≥ 1, meaning 𝒪_X is a torsion-free sheaf. It possesses no simple subobjects (skyscraper sheaves). The socle is strictly zero: Soc(𝒪_X) = 0.

  ### 2. Execution Limits

  • Can it start? No. Under the Categorical Machine, the target object 𝒪_X has a zero socle. The extraction machine is invalid and halts at step zero.
  • If it runs, does it finish? If we force the Ontological Sieve over the continuous moduli space, it starves. The loci where continuous Hodge structures align with discrete rational classes form a countable
  union of lower-dimensional subvarieties (Noether-Lefschetz loci).
  • Quantitative Friction Bounds: The search algorithm suffers from extreme information starvation. The discard rate is absolute: W → 1. Finding the discrete intersection requires evaluating uncountably many
  continuous states, and nearly all yield zero algebraic cycles.

  ### 3. The Alignment Diagnostic

  • Forcing a Continuous Gradient onto a Discrete Reality: The Hodge Conjecture uses the continuous analytic machinery of differential forms and complex deformations (the H^{k,k} bundle) to target a purely
  discrete, rigid reality (rational algebraic cycles in H²ᵏ(X, ℚ)). The structural alignment fails because continuous analytic geometry attempts to hunt down discrete topological artifacts.

  ### 4. The Structural Verdict

  • Paradox (Infinite Waste): The extraction mechanism fails on two fronts. Categorically, it hits the Zero-Socle Barrier. You cannot build a continuous space by greedily aggregating discrete points from the
  bottom up. Ontologically, it falls into the Infinite Waste Paradox. It evaluates an expanding continuous ocean of complex structures to find measure-zero discrete truths. The algorithm survives, but W → 1.
  It demands infinite continuous work to extract finite discrete truth.

  ### 5. The Resolution

  • To break the Zero-Socle Barrier: Abandon bottom-up subobject extraction. Points and discrete cycles cannot be injected into continuous spaces. Use top-down dual quotient filtration. Algebraic cycles must
  be defined via ideals and quotients (0 → ℐ_Y → 𝒪_X ↠ 𝒪_Y → 0).
  • To dismantle the Paradox: Realign the priority gradient to the native structural topology. Abandon the continuous analytic gradient (Hodge structures). Operate entirely within a discrete motivic framework,
  such as the Grothendieck ring of varieties K₀(X) or Chow motives. Here, algebraic cycles natively generate the space as a matroid, ensuring cardinality bloat c = 1 and discard rate W = 0.

  ### 6. "Why" Translation

  • Zero-Socle Barrier: Prove that the structure sheaf 𝒪_X of a variety with dim X ≥ 1 has no simple submodules. A simple object in Coh(X) is a skyscraper sheaf k(x) supported at a point. Because 𝒪_X is
  torsion-free, Hom(k(x), 𝒪_X) = 0. The continuous variety has no zero-dimensional base components to extract from the bottom up.
  • Infinite Waste Paradox: The space of complex structures forms a continuous moduli space. By the Baire Category Theorem, the Noether-Lefschetz locus (the set of complex structures where a generic Hodge
  class becomes rational) is a countable union of proper analytic subvarieties. These loci have Lebesgue measure zero. A continuous search or deformation algorithm almost surely misses them, forcing the search
  waste W to 1. The analytic space lacks the exchange property with the rational lattice.
